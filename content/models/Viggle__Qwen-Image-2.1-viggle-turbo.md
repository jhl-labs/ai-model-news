## 요약

**Built with Qwen.** A few-step distilled student of [Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1), trained by Viggle with Distribution Matching Distillation. It does both text-to-image and instruction-driven editing with 1–3 reference images in **6 transformer passes instead of 40**, with **no classifier-free guidance**.
