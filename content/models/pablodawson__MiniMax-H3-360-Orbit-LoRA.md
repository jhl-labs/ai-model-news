## 요약

**A LoRA for [MiniMax-H3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3) (FL2VA variant) that turns one photo into a frozen-time 360° camera orbit, and lands back on the exact frame it started from.**

Give the model the same image as the first and the last keyframe, and the LoRA orbits the camera all the way around the subject while the scene stays frozen. The clip closes on its own first frame, so orbits can be chained or stitched into longer shots without a visible seam.
