**Viggle-Animate replaces the character in a video with whatever you paint into one of its own frames** — motion, camera and timing untouched. You prepare that one frame in an image editor; from there the video stage runs no pose estimator, segmenter, face tracker or text encoder. Two inputs, three forward passes, 26 seconds a shot on one GPU.
