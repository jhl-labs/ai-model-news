## 요약

Open-license (Apache-2.0) 48 kHz voice isolation for noisy speech recordings and live audio.

Inworld Voice Isolator v1 ships as two models that share one architecture:

**Use them for** cleaning recordings before playback or publication, curating speech datasets and voice pipelines where the speaker's identity must survive. With the realtime model, use them for live calls and streams. Subtract the isolated voice from the input to extract background noise or a backing-track residual for vocals/music separation.
