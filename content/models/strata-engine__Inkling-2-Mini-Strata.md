## 요약

**Inkling 2 Mini** is Thinking Machines Lab's open-weights model made to run on local hardware. It is a looped transformer: the middle layers run three times for every token. It has 26.4B parameters and uses 3.4B per token (5.4B when the repeated passes are counted). It reads text and images, writes text, and supports up to 1M tokens of context (1,048,576).

**Pick by your graphics card's memory.** Each pack has a recommended card size. Smaller cards work too, from 4 GiB.
