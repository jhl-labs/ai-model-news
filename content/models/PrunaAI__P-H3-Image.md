## 요약

&nbsp;·&nbsp; Text-to-image and editing with up to 8 images

**P-H3-Image** is an image model for text-to-image generation and image editing, based on [MiniMax-H3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3). It renders 8-megapixel images in 8 steps without classifier-free guidance, edits with up to 8 input images, and comes with a prompt upsampler that turns a one-line request into the full prompt P-H3-Image renders best. It is strongest at photorealism, film and editorial looks, and graphic styles with short quoted text.
