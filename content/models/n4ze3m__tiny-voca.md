## 요약

tiny-voca is a small English text-to-speech model with two voices, F2 (female) and M2 (male). The complete model, including the waveform generator, has 4.5M parameters and was trained from scratch on about 18 hours of synthetic speech. It runs about 70x faster than real time on four CPU threads.
