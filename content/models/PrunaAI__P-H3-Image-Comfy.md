## 요약

&nbsp;·&nbsp; Text-to-image and editing with up to 8 images

This repository holds the ComfyUI files for **P-H3-Image**, an image model for text-to-image generation and image editing based on [MiniMax-H3](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3). It renders 8-megapixel images in 8 steps without CFG and edits with up to 8 input images. Every component comes in bf16 and in ComfyUI's native int8 and 4-bit formats. The smallest set is a 20 GB download that peaks at 21 GB of VRAM at 8 MP, against 143 GB of weights for MiniMax-H3.
