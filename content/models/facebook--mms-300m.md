## 요약

Facebook's MMS counting *300m* parameters.

MMS is Facebook AI's massive multilingual pretrained model for speech ("MMS"). It is pretrained in with [Wav2Vec2's self-supervised training objective](https://ai.facebook.com/blog/wav2vec-20-learning-the-structure-of-speech-from-raw-audio/) on about 500,000 hours of speech data in over 1,400 languages.

When using the model make sure that your speech input is sampled at 16kHz.
