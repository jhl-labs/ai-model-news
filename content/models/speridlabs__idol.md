## 요약

Idol turns one picture of an object into a 3D Gaussian splat. Give it a render, a generated image or a photo: it removes the background, frames the object and generates up to 1,048,576 Gaussians, written as a standard 3DGS `.ply`. It runs on consumer GPUs, using 7.2 GB of VRAM and can run in under 2 seconds.
