## 요약

Timestral is a **family of time series foundation models** for zero-shot forecasting, available in [**5M**](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-5M), [**28M**](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-28M), [**123M**](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-123M), and [**209M**](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-209M) parameter versions. This repository contains the 123M checkpoint.

For more information about our Timestral family, go to the main model page: [Timestral-209M](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Timestral-209M).
