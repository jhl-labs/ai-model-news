## 요약

MetricTrail estimates camera motion and per-frame metric depth from long RGB image sequences for visual odometry and 3D reconstruction research. It combines single-image metric depth, learned sparse correspondences, and local geometric optimization, with supplied or RGB-estimated intrinsics and optional rectified stereo. MetricTrail 1.0 was developed by NVIDIA.
