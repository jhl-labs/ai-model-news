## 요약

**Inkling 2 Mini Uncensored** is [Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini](https://huggingface.co/thinkingmachines/Inkling-2-Mini) (26B total, 3.4B active, text and image input, 1M token context) with its refusals removed by directional ablation (abliteration). It answers the requests the original refuses, in both chat and thinking mode, and keeps its MMLU: **78.95% → 79.47%**. Made by [OpenUncensored](https://openuncensored.com) on release day.
