## 요약

This repository contains the official implementation of *FastFrames: Video Generation at Interactive Latency*. We release [FastFrames-S](https://huggingface.co/apple/FastFrames-S) (1.3B) and [FastFrames-L](https://huggingface.co/apple/FastFrames-L) (14B). Both are autoregressive, two-step video diffusion models that generate 480p video at interactive latency. We also release an optimized version of LingBot 1.3B that is 16x faster than the official model and allows fluid camera-conditioned control.
