## 요약

**NVIDIA-Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B-BF16** is a small language model (SLM) further post-trained from [Gemma-4-E4B-it](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E4B-it) by NVIDIA. This is the full-precision (BF16) release. Nemotron-3.5-Bolt-E4B carries forward Gemma’s strong conversational capabilities and further improves multi-turn tool use, instruction following, and input grounding for fast local voice/device assistants, interactive personas, and embedded/edge interfaces. Designed for low-memory, low-TTFT operation, the model allows agents to respond quickly while preserving headroom for application…
