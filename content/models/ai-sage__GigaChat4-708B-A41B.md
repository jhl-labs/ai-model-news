## 요약

GigaChat 4 is the flagship hybrid model of the GigaChat family. It combines a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture with DeepSeek Sparse Attention (DSA) and Gated DeltaNet (GDN) with **708B** total parameters and **41B** active parameters per token. The model supports multilingual conversation, multi-step agentic tasks, tool calling, structured outputs, mathematical reasoning, coding, and question answering over long documents.
