## 요약

**Nemotron-3.5-Rerank-8B-BF16** was developed by NVIDIA for multilingual and multimodal question-answering retrieval. It is a world-class reranker optimized to produce a logit representing the relevance of a document to a text query. Documents may contain text, page images, or both, including charts, tables, and infographics. The model can be integrated into classical or agentic vision RAG pipelines. For text retrieval, the model was evaluated across 34 languages: English, Arabic, Assamese, Bengali, Bulgarian, Chinese, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Hindi, Hinglish, Indonesian, Italia…
